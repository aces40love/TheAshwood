A Grand Private Residence in Nashville's Most Coveted Enclave

Situated quietly on historic Ashwood Avenue between Belmont Boulevard and the vibrant 12 South avenue, The Ashwood represents the pinnacle of private residential hospitality. This is not a hotel suite or a commercial rental; it is a turnkey luxury home curated to the standards of the world’s most discerning travelers.

Behind the artisan cedar shake facade lies an expansive floor plan designed for effortless executive living, confidential meetings, and sophisticated entertaining. From the temperature-regulated wine service and chef's kitchen to the sun-drenched second-floor executive loft, every square foot is tailored for productive work and profound rest.

Private Residencies

How You May Stay

The Ashwood is offered only for stays of 30 days and longer. Choose the primary residence or the guesthouse, then decide whether chauffeur service travels with you. A culinary package may be added to any of the four.

Primary Residence

The main estate as a private home. Suites, chef's kitchen, living rooms, pool, and veranda, prepared for an extended stay of thirty days or more.

Primary Residence + Transportation

The main estate with Maybach Sprinter chauffeur service for airport arrivals, city appointments, and evening engagements.

Guesthouse Rental

The detached carriage house on its own. Two bedrooms, kitchenette, and private entry across the courtyard, for a quieter independent stay.

Guesthouse Rental + Transportation

The carriage house with chauffeur service, for a smaller footprint that still arrives and moves through Nashville with ease.

Available With Every Stay

Culinary Package

A private chef and a kitchen provisioned to your palate, arranged before you arrive. This addition may be joined to any of the four residencies above.
